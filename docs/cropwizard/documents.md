# CropWizard Documents

The CropWizard document database consists of over 400,000 publications, blogs, newsletters, and articles from three categories of sources:

1. **Extension websites** of over 55 US land-grant universities — the largest category.
2. A small number of similar documents from **other universities** in the US and abroad.
3. A growing set of **open-access research publications** from scientific journals relevant to agriculture.

## Academic journals

- **Springer Nature (open access)** — journals including *Crop Health*, *Animal Cognition*, *Botanical Studies*, *Chemical and Biological Technologies in Agriculture*, *Horticulture Advances*, *Rice*, and *Agricultural and Food Economics*, published under CC BY (and CC0) licenses.
- **Wiley (open access)** — nearly 20,000 articles across ~75 journals such as *New Phytologist*, *Plant Biotechnology Journal*, *Evolutionary Applications*, *Agronomy Journal*, *Crop Science*, *GCB Bioenergy*, and *Methods in Ecology and Evolution*, under CC BY, CC BY-NC, and CC BY-NC-ND licenses.

For the complete license breakdown per journal, see [Document Licenses](licenses.md).
